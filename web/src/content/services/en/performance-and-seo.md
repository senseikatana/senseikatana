## What problem it solves

A website can look good and still lose visits: it takes too long to paint, breaks hydration or has three URLs for the same content. The problem is rarely just "SEO".

I work on the measurable causes: JavaScript weight, images, fonts, rendering and link structure. Then we decide what to optimise with data, not intuition.

## How I approach it

- **Measure before touching**: a baseline of Core Web Vitals and the real funnel.
- **Performance budget**: every route has a ceiling; if it exceeds it, it does not ship.
- **Correct technical SEO**: one canonical URL per language, consistent `hreflang`, sitemap and metadata.
- **No ranking promises**: ranking depends on factors I do not control. What I can control is the technical base and the measurement.

## Who it is not for

It is not for someone looking to guarantee the first spot on Google. That is not an honest deliverable, and I do not sell it that way.
